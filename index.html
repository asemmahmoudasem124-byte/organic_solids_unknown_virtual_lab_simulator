1. Solubility & Litmus Paper Test (All 15 Solids are Soluble in H₂O)

Dissolve 1 spatula unknown in 2 mL Distilled H₂O & dip Blue/Red Litmus:

  • Acidic (Blue Litmus → Red): Oxalic acid or Tartaric acid → Confirm with Solid Na₂CO₃ (brisk effervescence) and differentiate via Conc. H₂SO₄ on hot.
  • Neutral (No Litmus change): Urea or Salts of acids (Na⁺, K⁺, NH₄⁺ formate, acetate, oxalate, tartarate) → Proceed to Action of FeCl₃.
2. Action of FeCl₃ (For Neutral Unknowns)

Recipe: 2 spatulas unknown + 2 mL H₂O + 3 drops FeCl₃

Blood Red Color

Formate or Acetate salts.
Diff: Alkaline KMnO₄
• Decolorization + Brown ppt → Formate (Confirm: HgCl₂ + dil. HCl + boil → white ppt)
• No change → Acetate

Lemon Yellow Color

Oxalate or Tartarate salts.
Diff: Conc. H₂SO₄ on hot
• Eff. & clear solution → Oxalate
• Eff. & charring → Tartarate (Confirm: Fenton's test → violet)

No Action (Blank)

Urea.
Confirm: Biuret Test
1 sp dry unknown → Melt & resolidify → Cool → 2–3 mL 10% NaOH + 1–2 drops CuSO₄ → Purple color.

3. Basic Radical Identification (For all 12 Salts: Na⁺, K⁺, NH₄⁺)
  • Cold 30% NaOH Test: 2 spatulas solid + 2 mL 30% NaOH (on cold / shake) → Pungent Ammonia (NH₃) odor (turns red litmus blue) = NH₄⁺. No ammonia odor = Na⁺ or K⁺.
  • Dry Heat / Flame Test: Heat dry unknown strongly on flame → Golden yellow flame = Na⁺; Violet (lilac) flame = K⁺.
